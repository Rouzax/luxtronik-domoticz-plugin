*[COP]: Coefficient of Performance: heat delivered divided by electricity used. A COP of 4 means 4 kWh of heat for 1 kWh of electricity.
*[DHW]: Domestic hot water: the tap water tank the heat pump heats.
*[HUP]: Heizungsumwälzpumpe: the heating circulation pump that moves water through the heating circuit.
*[VBO]: Ventilator / Brunnen- oder Soleumwälzpumpe: the brine (source) pump that moves fluid through the ground loop.
*[superheat]: How many degrees the refrigerant gas is above its boiling point when it leaves the evaporator. Too low risks liquid reaching the compressor; too high wastes capacity.
*[brine]: The antifreeze fluid in the ground loop that carries heat from the ground to the heat pump.
